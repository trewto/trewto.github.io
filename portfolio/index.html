Research

M.Sc. thesis · BUET · September 2026

Generative Artificial Intelligence Based Modelling of Pedestrian Group Crossing Behaviour at Intersections in Dhaka City

Research focus: Traffic-video processing, pedestrian group context, crossing-intention modelling, and trajectory forecasting.

My contributions

  • Research datasets Created vehicle and pedestrian annotation datasets, plus calibrated ground-plane pedestrian trajectories for Dhaka’s mixed traffic.
  • Custom research tools Built human-in-the-loop annotation software and interactive homography-calibration tools to prepare and spatially align traffic data.
  • Video-to-data pipeline Fine-tuned YOLO detectors; integrated tracking, camera synchronisation, coordinate conversion, data cleaning, behavioural labelling, and feature construction.
  • Prediction models Implemented crossing-intention classifiers and LSTM/CVAE trajectory forecasting, including generative modelling of multiple plausible future trajectories.
Methods & toolsModel architectures, tracking methods, and feature construction
  • Detection & tracking: Fine-tuned YOLO detectors; ByteTrack for vehicles and BoT-SORT with re-identification for pedestrians.
  • Calibration: GPS-assisted homography for ground-plane coordinates, with active-area filtering.
  • Pedestrian attributes: Appearance-based age-group and gender estimates, stabilized per track with unknown categories retained.
  • Traffic & group context: Nearby-vehicle features and density-based clustering (DBSCAN) group snapshots derived from pedestrian positions.
  • Crossing-intention classifiers: Logistic regression, support vector machines (RBF-SVM), Random Forest, and a binary conditional variational autoencoder (CVAE).
  • Trajectory forecasting: Long short-term memory (LSTM) and CVAE models for forecasting pedestrian trajectories.
